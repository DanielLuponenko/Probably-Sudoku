## Resolved Engine findings

1. **Shredder near-full deadlock — fixed:** new fouls cannot consume the final free blank. A carried fully fouled board gets one deterministic opening at its next Turn; other expiry values remain intact. Wording now says “up to three” and discloses the free-blank exception.
2. **Gray/Garry concentrated-unit deadlock — fixed:** a candidate must leave a blank outside the chosen row/box; otherwise that Turn has no grey unit. Repeated-turn and final-placement regressions cover both Bosses.
3. **Executive Editor standing-item mismatch — fixed:** only Bookmarks with event hooks can sleep. Standing Hand/Turn/Toss/Clue/payout upgrades remain active and are not falsely selected. No cards, stored puzzle budgets or permanent item benefits are removed. New policy takes effect on the next ordinary Boss Turn, without rewriting a saved current Turn.
4. **Mirror Full Clear coverage — added:** the actual final-square regression includes three suppressed line scores plus preserved Full Clear item contributions. No Mirror rule change was needed.
5. **Standing-rule action-depth — added:** three real-deal/action tests exercise Editor Hand count/conservation, Deadline exhaustion/rescue-decline persistence, and Erratum Toss rejection with exact no-mutation proof.

The new square restrictions guarantee an unbarred blank at a Turn boundary, not that every held digit has a legal move. Existing Obstacle/Hand restrictions still apply. These targeted cases do not exhaust every nearly-full-board shape or inventory permutation.

## Shared evidence and limits

- `FinalBossTests.testEntireBookRouteReservesExactlyFiveBossesForLevelNine` samples 128 seeded full routes, checks all five final Bosses stay in Level 9 and Collector does not join that pool. `testFinalBossOnlyAppearsInLastPuzzleAndMatchesItsAnnouncement` checks Level 9's two ordinary puzzles have no Boss.
- `ProductionCombinationMatrixTests.testAllBooksBossesAndObstaclesKeepRulesConservationAndRescueIdentity` covers 12×19×9 = 2,052 cached-board combinations with real placement where legal, Buff attempts, End Turn, save/restore, and exactly-once rescue. Its broad invariants are valuable but do not prove every item permutation, all near-full boards or all exact Boss-specific score amounts.
- `BossBoardVisualTests` checks all 19 unique signatures, visible distinct 24pt seals, phase 0 vs quarter-cycle differences, unchanged glyph regions for all 81 cells, square route art, Reduce Motion, disabled ambient motion and paused-clock behavior.
- Motion is procedural paper-and-ink illustration, not 19 separate 3D characters/cutscenes. Most standing-Boss motion is a rule-themed ambient signature. Actual location-sensitive feedback is tied to real engine state for Censor, Fog, Shredder and Gray restrictions; inventory feedback identifies the actual sleeping/barred card.
- Each small `BossPerimeterVignette` owns its own TimelineView/Canvas. It pauses for inactive scene, covered presentation/page flip, reduced motion, disabled background motion and Low Power Mode. No per-frame clock is published into the board or engine.
