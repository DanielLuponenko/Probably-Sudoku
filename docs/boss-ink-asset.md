Preserve the reference's flat matte India ink soaked into paper: subtle uneven warm-black translucent washes, fine dry-brush fibres, splattered dots hugging the edges, a few fine diagonal dragged ink streaks especially upper-right. NOT burned parchment, NOT charcoal rock, NOT raised or cracked material, NO smoke blur, NO shadows. Center is nearly opaque warm black and fairly calm so cream numbers can be laid over it. Shape is a compact portrait rectangle width:height 0.86, with ragged ink edges, not a tall strip. Ink fills 94% canvas width and 94% height; tiny transparent margins, preserve fine edge splatter.
