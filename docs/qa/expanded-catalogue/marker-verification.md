Setup used the existing QA menu and its simultaneous-clear scoring fixture; all tested placements and choices used actual game controls.

- **Fork:** accessible Inspect marker showed the compact canonical explanation, kept the selected five, and dismissed without placing. Evidence: `native-pledge-before-charge.png`, `native-pledge-paid.png`.
- **Exchange:** actual placement produced choices for nine and two threes with distinct UUIDs. Cancel preserved all three card identities and four Tosses.
