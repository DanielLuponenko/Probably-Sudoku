# Native Marker choice checks

Use an isolated QA Book, with iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone SE at normal text, then SE at accessibility text size 5. Keep the saved real Book untouched. Use actual QA grants and actual Hand placements; never manufacture a successful screenshot by changing the score. Capture the pending choice and resolved board for each case.

## Fork

1. Give Fork a blank coordinate reachable by a Hand card; leave at least two cards in the Pool. Play the correct card there.
2. The paper choice shows both drawn numbers, and has no Cancel after revealing them. Before selecting, the choice, board, Hand and Turn stay stationary.
3. Background/reopen while the choice is pending. The same two choices return; no second draw occurs. If both digits match, they remain two separate choice buttons.
4. Choose one and confirm. Exactly one card joins the Hand; the other returns to the Pool. Repeated taps cannot add another card. End Turn must not have run underneath the slip.

## Pledge

1. Have at least two coins after any Boss placement fee; give Pledge a playable blank. Attempt the correct placement.
2. Before deciding, the card remains in the Hand, the square remains blank and the balance stays unchanged. The slip identifies the +100 Points / two-coin trade.
3. Choose Place normally (and separately Cancel). Each resolves the original ordinary placement once, without the trade's coin charge or extra Points.
4. Repeat on another Pledge square and accept. The original placement completes once, adds 100 placement Points and charges exactly two coins. The live score and source receipt agree. Rapid Confirm taps cannot repeat the trade.

## Exchange

1. Place on Exchange with duplicate digits remaining in the Hand. Confirm that every selectable card has its own choice and Boss-blocked cards are excluded (unless that exact card has Release).
2. Cancel first: every card and its identity remain; no use is charged. Repeat on another Exchange square and select the second duplicate.
3. Exactly that card returns to the Pool. The replacement has a different digit and a new identity; the other duplicate stays. No ordinary Toss charge is spent.
4. If Clean Finish captured the returned card, its challenge fails. The saved choice cannot be replayed after resolution.

## Rebind / claim relocation

1. Grant a Rebind Buff and two Marker types with untriggered, blank, unbarred claims. Open Rebind. Filled, given-covered, barred and already-triggered claims must not be legal source targets.
2. Pick one claim, then inspect legal destination targets. Cancel before final confirmation: Buff, claim, entitlement, coins, Hand and board stay unchanged.
3. Confirm a blank unowned destination. Consume exactly that Rebind copy and move the complete claim. Its old position becomes unmarked; the destination has its original Marker explanation and stable claim history.
4. If available, repeat with a historical Patina claim on the next Puzzle; its earned growth survives the move. A Transposition swaps complete records between different Marker types and cannot reset their spent state.
